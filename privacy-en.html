About Fergetider

This policy covers Fergetider on Android phones and tablets and in vehicles running Android Automotive OS. Phone-projected Android Auto is not implemented in the version reviewed. The Fergetider developer is the controller for the app’s processing. The app has no accounts, ads, or integrated analytics or crash reporting tools.

Location and Entur

With permission, the app uses approximate or precise foreground location to find nearby ferry stops and service alerts and to centre the map. Location-based requests to Entur can include the bounds of an area around your location or the visible map. Entur also receives search text, including what you type while suggestions appear, and identifiers for stops, routes or departures. This enables search, timetables, service alerts and real-time information. You can revoke location access in Android settings and continue to search manually.

Entur AS is the controller for its own API logs. Entur says it logs IP addresses, request paths and the app identifier for operations, capacity planning, security and abuse prevention, based on legitimate interests. It retains these logs for 14 months, with longer retention possible for security or incident investigations. Its terms give no separate retention period for search text or coordinates in requests. Entur API · Entur · Personvern

Maps

The app requests map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation through osmdroid. The server receives your IP address, the app identifier and the tiles requested. The tiles reveal the displayed area, including when the map is centred on you. The OpenStreetMap Foundation may use technical logs for operations, security and planning. OpenStreetMap Foundation · Privacy · Tile Usage Policy

Storage and diagnostics

Favourites, settings and cached maps are stored on your device. The mobile app keeps a local troubleshooting log that can include timestamps, search text, stop and route identifiers, permission status, map centre coordinates and errors. The log resets when it exceeds about 256 KiB. You can clear it in the diagnostics screen or choose to copy, save or share it. The app does not upload this log automatically. You can clear local app data in Android settings. Android can back up eligible app data, including preferences and local files, or transfer it to another device, depending on your device and settings. Android Auto Backup

Google Play

Google handles distribution through Google Play. If you opt in to sharing usage and diagnostics with Google, Android Vitals can provide the developer with technical reports, such as crashes, app freezes and performance information. These help investigate problems and improve stability. Google · Privacy · Android Vitals
